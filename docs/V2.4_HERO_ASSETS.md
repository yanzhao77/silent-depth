# V2.4 — Hero Asset & Submarine Readability

> Part of the SILENT DEPTH V2.4 → V3.0 visual roadmap. Focus: the V2.2.5 QA
> problems *"submarine is a black silhouette"* and *"enemy ships are unreadable"*.

## Submarine — "black but readable"

The V2.3 procedural fallback (`src/renderer/procedural/submarineGeometry.ts`) is the
unit that actually renders today (GLB hero assets remain the long-term target and are
loaded when their approved LOD set is present; the procedural unit is the all-or-
nothing fallback). V2.4 retunes that fallback so the boat reads as a warship at night
without being self-lit:

- Base colours stay **dark**; emissive floor reduced to a near-zero crush-prevention
  value (hull `0x22323b` @ `emissiveIntensity 0.05`, down from `0.16`).
- **Wet specular** carried by low roughness + high metalness on the pressure hull and
  conning tower (roughness 0.34 / metalness 0.72).
- **Fresnel rim** (`addFresnelRim`) adds a cold moon/sky rim on the hull, tower, fins,
  periscope and waterline. This is a presentation-only `onBeforeCompile` injection; it
  separates the silhouette from the night sea *without raising base brightness* —
  exactly the spec's *"Dark but readable"* requirement.

The structural silhouette parts (pressure-hull, conning-tower, periscope-shaft,
rudder, five-blade-propeller, forward-torpedo-tube) are unchanged and still validated
by `render-asset-registry.test.ts`, so recognition at 50/100/300 m is preserved.

## Enemy ship readability (consumed, not authored here)

V2.4 does **not** modify ship geometry or silhouettes — that is the V2.2 deliverable
and remains intact. What V2.4 adds is that every *already-visible* ship now throws a
correct, class-scaled wake (see `V2.4_OCEAN.md`), so the player can read a Destroyer
or Tanker by its **wake signature** at 150–300 m, reinforcing the silhouette work.

## Honesty note

Per the roadmap's anti-"fake completion" rules: the material and rim changes are
**IMPLEMENTED and unit-tested at the contract level**, but **not yet browser-verified
on target hardware**. No GLB binaries were generated; the procedural fallback —
which is the asset that currently renders — was improved in place. Simulation, AI,
sonar, physics and mission code were **not touched**.
